================================================================
7) KEY NUMERICAL FINDINGS
================================================================
  - No single candidate PDE fits the synthetic field (best 0.684; pure wave 0.864). The field is a dispersive pulse launched from U(0)=0, U_t(0)=odd bump, peaking at t~3 (Umax~1.51) then decaying.
  - The c2=1 wave residual is large everywhere in the interior (87.9% of points |r|>0.1) and is NOT a spatial-boundary effect (boundary r=0).
  - Wave c2 estimates are unstable across splits (0.064-0.076) and fail holdout (rel_resid 0.82-0.90).
  - The experimental profile is best modeled by reaction-diffusion (rel_resid ~0.41-0.54) with a positive mean growth rate ~0.025-0.029 /h; channels are similar but not identical (rel_diff 0.15-0.23).

================================================================
8) ISSUES / LIMITATIONS
================================================================
  - Bugs fixed: (1) spatial-derivative index error in holdout; (2) 1-D vs 2-D in phase lstsq; (3) zero-intercept growth fit giving a spurious negative slope.
  - rel_resid is a single scalar per candidate and can understate fit quality; no candidate clears the 0.3 bar, so "no good PDE" is the honest conclusion rather than a specific one.
  - The 5-point-stencil wave fit was initially degenerate (c2~-7.7e-13, rel_resid=1.0) because the 5-point "second derivative" was coded with first-derivative coefficients. It was corrected to the O(dx^4) central 5-point second derivative (-U[i+2]+16U[i+1]-30U[i]+16U[i-1]-U[i-2])/(12h^2) fit over the common interior [2:-2,2:-2]; it now returns c2=0.075497, rel_resid=0.8682, consistent with output/robustness.py.
  - Experimental analysis uses only 5 time points, so second-in-time derivatives are very coarse; PDE fits on the experimental field are therefore low-confidence.
  - The experimental space grid is sparse (38 points, 50-unit spacing) and the coordinate_definitions.json explicitly says not to invent a physical space unit; all experimental "D" coefficients are in source-assay/hour^2 units.
  - Fourier phase fits are sensitive to noise at low amplitude (high-k modes); phase_resid>1 for most modes.
