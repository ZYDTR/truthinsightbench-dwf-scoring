=== (iv) Mechanism: why c06 = c01*c05 improves a LINEAR fit ===
c05 ~ poly(c01, 1): residual variance fraction = 1.435e-03  (coeffs descending: [0.006343 0.372136])
c05 ~ poly(c01, 2): residual variance fraction = 1.527e-05  (coeffs descending: [2.190075e-05 5.390834e-03 3.795015e-01])
c05 ~ poly(c01, 3): residual variance fraction = 1.527e-05  (coeffs descending: [9.269460e-11 2.189481e-05 5.390937e-03 3.795011e-01])
=> density carries essentially NO information beyond a quadratic in depth (residual 1.5e-5).
   Therefore c06 = c01*c05 ~ a0*c01 + a1*c01^2 + a2*c01^3 is a CONSTRAINED CUBIC in c01,
   and 'linear in c06' is a 2-parameter constrained cubic in c01. The improvement from
   R2 0.99138 (linear c01) to 0.99905 is curvature, delivered by the fixed shape of the
   density ramp, not by new chronological information in the density column.
